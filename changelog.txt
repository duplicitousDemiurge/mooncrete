    - Added individual unlock techs instead of inserting into pre-existing Muluna techs
    - Added hazard mooncrete/hazard refined mooncrete
    - Patch for dectorio flooring
    - Added alumina gravel with Dectorio gravel on
    - If gravel variants is added, replaces Muluna crushed-gravel as its own "regolith gravel" item
    - Patch for electrified tiles
    - Patch for crushing industry
    - Settings, more settings with Dectorio and/or Electric Tiles installed
